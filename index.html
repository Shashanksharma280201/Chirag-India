What people get wrong

Three myths that stop pledges

✕

“Eye donation disfigures the face.”

False. Only the cornea, a small clear layer at the front, is taken. The face looks the same.

✕

“Any age is too old to donate.”

False. Even people aged 90+ can donate.

✕

“Only good eyesight can be donated.”

False. Even spectacle-wearers qualify. Cataract or previous surgery is often fine too.

Tell your family today

A pledge card means nothing if your family says no later. In India, the family's consent is what allows donation to happen. Have the conversation now, not later — tell your spouse, children or siblings that this is what you want.

03Within 6 hrs

When the time comes

Within 6 hours of death, the family should:

  • Close the eyelids gently
  • Raise the head slightly with a pillow
  • Switch off fans; keep the AC running if available
  • Place wet cotton or a clean wet cloth over the closed eyes, if possible
  • Call your nearest eye bank or call us — we connect you

FAQ

Questions people ask us

Who can donate?
Anyone. Age, eyesight and spectacles don't matter. People with diabetes, hypertension, asthma, or those who have had cataract surgery can usually donate too.
Does it disfigure the face?
No. Only the cornea — the small, clear front layer of the eye — is removed. The face is not affected and the eyes look natural afterwards.
How long does it take?
20–30 minutes, at home or in the hospital. The eye bank team comes to you. It does not delay funeral arrangements.
Is there a cost?
No. Eye donation is completely free. Nobody should ever ask the family for money.
Can I donate my own eyes while I'm alive?
No. Eyes can only be donated after death. What you can do today is pledge, and tell your family.
How many people can one donor help?
Two. Each cornea can restore sight to one person, so one donor can give sight to two people.
Is Chirag India an eye bank?
No. We are an awareness initiative. We register pledges, spread the word, and when a family calls us, we connect them to the nearest registered eye bank within hours.
