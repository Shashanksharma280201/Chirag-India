What people get wrong

Three myths that stop pledges

✕

“Eye donation disfigures the face.”

False. Only the cornea, a small clear layer at the front, is taken. The face looks the same.

✕

“Any age is too old to donate.”

False. Even people aged 90+ can donate.

✕

“Only good eyesight can be donated.”

False. Even spectacle-wearers qualify. Cataract or previous surgery is often fine too.

Who we are

We're not a hospital. We're not an eye bank. We are a small bridge between the family of the deceased and the nearest eye bank.

We run eye donation awareness in schools, housing societies, corporates and old age homes. We are there for more than six years. Our previous website chiragindia.co.in was in existence since 2021 but because of a technical glitch, we couldn't continue and migrated to the new one.

Donate your eyes

It takes 2 minutes to pledge.

01Today

Pledge now

Takes 2 minutes — online on the NOTTO Donor Pledge Portal, or on a manual form from your nearest eye bank. You get a donor card. A pledge costs nothing and you can withdraw it at any time.

However, the family of the deceased can donate eyes even without a prior pledge.

NOTTO Donor Pledge Portal ↗
02Today

Tell your family today

A pledge card means nothing if your family says no later. In India, the family's consent is what allows donation to happen. Have the conversation now, not later — tell your spouse, children or siblings that this is what you want.

03Within 6 hrs

When the time comes — call the nearest eye bank

Family members have to call the nearest eye bank as early as possible after the demise, giving a royal salute to the deceased. Remember, the entire process has to take place within 6 hours from the time of death. Meanwhile:

  • Close the eyelids gently
  • Raise the head slightly with a pillow
  • Switch off fans; keep the AC running if available
  • Place wet cotton or a clean wet cloth over the closed eyes, if possible
  • Call your nearest eye bank as early as possible and become a Karmayogi
Emergency helpline (NOTTO, toll-free)
or the contact number of your nearest eye bank

A trained eye bank team comes to your home or hospital. The procedure takes 20–30 minutes and does not delay the funeral.

FAQ

Questions people ask us

Who can donate?
Anyone. Age, eyesight and spectacles don't matter. People with diabetes, hypertension, asthma, or those who have had cataract surgery can usually donate too.
Does it disfigure the face?
No. Only the cornea — the small, clear front layer of the eye — is removed. The face is not affected and the eyes look natural afterwards.
How long does it take?
20–30 minutes, at home or in the hospital. The eye bank team comes to you. It does not delay funeral arrangements.
Is there a cost?
No. Eye donation is completely free. Nobody should ever ask the family for money.
Can I donate my own eyes while I'm alive?
No. Eyes can only be donated after death. What you can do today is pledge, and tell your family.
How many people can one donor help?
Two. Each cornea can restore sight to one person, so one donor can give sight to two people.
Is Chirag Indians an eye bank?
No. We are an awareness initiative. Through various ways we promote the eye donation campaign, requesting people to call the nearest eye bank after the death of their beloved one. Our drive also includes pledges for eye donation. We have succeeded so far and finally hit the bull's eye: a few families of the deceased have actually donated the eyes of their beloved one.
